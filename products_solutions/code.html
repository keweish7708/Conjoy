  • Core/Cladding: 9/125 μm
  • Wavelength: 1310nm / 1550nm
  • Distance: Long Range (Up to 10km - 100km)
  • Attenuation: Very Low (~0.35 dB/km @1310nm)

  • Core/Cladding Diameter: 50/125 μm
  • Operating Wavelength: 850 nm / 1300 nm
  • Transmission Distance: Short Range (Up to 300m - 550m)
  • Attenuation: Higher (~3.0 dB/km @850nm)

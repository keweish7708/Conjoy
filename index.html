About Us

Your Trusted Partner for
Data Center Power Solutions

Conjoy Innovation Technology is registered in Texas, USA, and specializes in providing professional power equipment and system integration engineering services for data centers and computing centers in the North American market. Our business covers the integration of core power equipment such as power generation, transformation, and voltage regulation, and we also provide OEM/ODM cabinets, racks, optical fibers, and other supporting materials.
